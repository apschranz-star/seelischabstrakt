Governance

Built legally sound, from GDPR to the AI Act

  • GDPR by design

    Legal bases and information duties (Art. 13/14) as mandatory fields in the data model, deletion concepts, per-record disclosure capability.

  • Lawful outreach

    Channel choice before campaign: Austrian TKG, German UWG. Compliance decides the process, not the other way round.

  • License-checked data

    Every source checked before use: ODbL vs CC-BY, attribution, share-alike. No silent licensing risks in the product.

  • EU AI Act

    Transparency and documentation duties carried along from the start, not retrofitted.

  • Audit trails

    Every change logged, every number recomputable. Auditability is a feature, not an add-on.

Built for the conservative market: runs locally without forced cloud, open data sources, German-language documentation, handover without lock-in, pilot to full scale without changing architecture.
