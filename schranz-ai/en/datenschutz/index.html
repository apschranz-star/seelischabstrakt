Privacy

This website stores no cookies and runs no analytics. It sets one value in your browser's local storage, the language you chose, and nothing else. That value never leaves your device.

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you open it, the host processes your IP address in its server logs, as any web server does. GitHub's privacy statement applies to that processing.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser sends a request to Google's servers for the font files, which transmits your IP address. Legal basis is the legitimate interest in a consistent presentation (Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR).

If you write an email, the data you send is used only to answer you. You have the rights to access, rectification, erasure and restriction under the GDPR, and the right to complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority.
